## 2. Every cable routed through named runs

The wiring model (`cad/fitout/wiring.py`) used to draw each cable as a line between anchor points, which cut through parts in places. It is now a **route network**:
- **33 runs** (R01–R33). Each has a containment, a fixing method and a cross-section.
- **Packing:** each run packs its cables in rows, biggest first. Twin cables are two cores side by side, so the 3D model shows red + and black − separately.
- **Run lengths and fill** come from the model. Conduit sizes are chosen at ≤ 50 % fill (split conduit, cables laid in).
- **Route check:** `cad/cable_check.py` tests every cable against every solid part every 2.5 mm. It found **139** places where a cable passed through a part before this change. It now finds **0**, in both travel and camp.
- **In the viewer:** a new layer, *Cable routes*, shows the conduits (see-through), P-clips every 300 mm and grommets.

**Where the cables go:**

| Zone | Runs | How |
|---|---|---|
| Front bay | R01–R06, R31 | Riser in the gap between the busbars and battery 1, then the 32 mm rear channel behind the board edge and the inverter. The front channel connects the chargers to their MIDI fuses. The charging-board loom runs under the chargers' terminal faces. The bundle leaves over the inverter, above the drawer carcass. |
| Tub tray | R07, R08 | The tray is widened to 120 mm, with a **divider**: 230 V outboard, 12 V inboard. |
| Wall channel and gland plate | R09, R10, R32 | 22 mm channel behind the drawer runner panel. The old outlet hole gets a gland plate with **one 230 V grommet row and two 12 V rows**. |
| Rear cabinet | R11–R13, R33 | 12 V enters through a 100 × 22 slot with a split grommet. 230 V has its own gland beside the RCBO enclosure. A corridor runs beside the enclosure, and a vertical loom runs behind the rear panel. |
| Outside | R14–R21 | 12 V in black NW48 split conduit; 230 V in a separate orange 25 mm conduit. Both go down the wall cavity, under the floor and along the rail web on P-clips into M6 rivnuts (no welding or drilling the flanges). Branches go to the cabin (floor grommet) and the engine bay. |
| Gap and roof | R22–R26 | Separate 230 V and 12 V risers to the junction box, which now has an **internal barrier**. A 32 mm conduit runs up the cab back, then cables go **inside the hollow platform rear end member** to Starlink, the PV branch and U11. |
| Tent | R27, R28 | Harness split into **T1A (230 V)** and **T1D (12 V + PV)**. **T2** now crosses to the base at the **front hinge**, so opening the shell can't pull on it. |
| Power slide | R29, R30 | Trunk on the glide tray, then the chain, with a separator between AC3 and DO2. |

## 4. Electrical corrections (design audit)

| Issue found | Change |
|---|---|
| The visor panel (a higher-voltage class) was in parallel with PV1/PV2, with no string fuses | **Visor on its own MPPT 100/20 (U13)**, on the passenger face of the distribution board. The roof MPPT goes back to a **100/30**. With two panels in parallel, no string fuses are needed. |
| PVR 6 mm²: 6 % voltage drop | **10 mm²** solar pair (about 2.5 %) |
| DO5 + tent harness 6 mm² on a 30 A fuse: 11 % drop | **10 mm² / 20 A** (about 3.7 % at full fuse rating) |
| RB1/RB2/SH1/SH2 70 mm²: both 300 A MRBFs in parallel can feed 600 A into them | **120 mm²** |
| AC1 2.5 mm²: with PowerAssist the AC-out carries about 25 A | **4 mm²** and a **2-pole main switch Q0** (AS/NZS 3001.2) in the RCBO enclosure |
| Shore RCBO Q4 was single-pole | **2-pole RCBO** (campsite polarity is unknown) |
| 230 V shared conduit, gland, tray, chain and harness with 12 V | **Segregated everywhere** (see section 2) |
| AC earth reached the chassis only through the DC negative bus | **PE2:** 6 mm² green/yellow from the RCBO earth bar to the chassis bond |
| DCF 25 mm²: 3.9 % drop at 100 A | **35 mm²** |
| VIS 6 mm²: 4.6 % drop | **10 mm²**. U11 gets **per-channel fuses** (M2 15 A, M3/M4 7.5 A, latches 3 A, logic 2 A). |
| Visor latches are rated for short duty (about 5 s) but were held through the whole deploy | Energised only until B1 opens. On stow they spring into ramped strikers with no power. |
| VSW had no common wire | **4-core** |
| Starlink: a 10 A fuse doesn't protect the barrel lead | **7.5 A** |
| Pod Anderson X3 was unfused behind 30 A | **F14 20 A** in FB2 |
| U7 → U8 USB-C link not in the schedule | **DO1E**, 4 mm² |
| The QS1 note said the system side goes dead | BB3 stays live from the chargers. **Open QP1–QP3, S2 off and ignition off before QS1.** |
| ALT − was landed on the battery post | Land it on the **body earth**, on the chassis side of the battery sensor |
| Smart-switching proposal: the rocker feed was unfused, and 12 V would have gone into a Cerbo logic input | 2 A inline fuse on the tap; dry contact to ground (`smart-switching.md`) |
| Battery pairs were not the same length | Each pair is cut to the longer length (the schedule shows this) |

The Class-T sits beside BB3, so the CT1 link is under 200 mm and counts as fused at the source.
