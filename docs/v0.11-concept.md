# v0.11 concept: slide-out windscreen solar visor

Status: **eleventh iteration, concept.** The interference check reports **0 clashes** (travel + camp). The roof crown, platform height and bar positions are still `[EST]`; the visor needs the roof measurements below before anything is ordered.

- Renders: [`renders/v0.11/`](../renders/v0.11) (deployed, stowed, side, mechanism, stowed section)
- Wiring: new sheet **E9 Visor drive** in [`docs/wiring/`](wiring), plus changes on E1, E3, E4 and E6

## 1. What it does

A ~250 W panel (PV5, ~1300 × 1000 mm) lives flat under the roof platform. Parked, one button at the rear panel slides it forward out of the platform front and tilts it down to **20°** in front of the windscreen. The same button (other direction) brings it back and locks it for travel.

| | |
|---|---|
| Solar, parked | **770 W**: roof 320 W + visor 250 W + tent 200 W |
| Solar, driving | 520 W (the visor is stowed) |
| Stowed | flat in the ~70 mm layer between the roof skin and the platform frame, latched at the front |
| Deployed | hinge ~70 mm ahead of the windscreen header, panel tilted 20° toward the bonnet |

## 2. Mechanism

| Part | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Guide rails** (×2) | Aluminium profile rails with wheel carriages, hung under the platform at Y ±560 on 40 × 5 straps | Stiff in every direction, no holes in the roof |
| **Slide drive M2** | One 12 V worm gearmotor at the rear cross-member drives a cross shaft; HTD belts on each side pull both carriages | One shaft means both sides move together, so the panel can't rack and jam. A worm can't be back-driven, so the panel can't creep |
| **Hinge beam + arms** | RHS 50 × 25 across the carriages; arms reach 235 mm forward to the hinge | Puts the hinge in front of the windscreen header, so the tilting panel clears the roof |
| **Tilt drives M3, M4** | Self-locking worm rotary actuators at each end of the hinge beam | They hold any angle with the power off and don't move in wind |
| **Hard stop** | Mechanical stop at **22°** | Even if a limit switch or a drive fails, the panel can't reach the glass |
| **Front latches Y1, Y2** | 12 V solenoid latches, spring-locked, powered only to release | **Fail locked**: losing power or a wire leaves the panel locked in for travel |
| **Bumper** | Rubber strip on the panel front edge | Last line of defence for the paint and glass |
| **Manual override** | 8 mm hex on the M2 shaft end and on both tilt drives; latch pull-release | Stow it with a drill if the controller or the power fails |

## 3. Clearances (model, measured with OCC `distance`)

| Tilt | To the cab body / glass | Front edge above the bonnet |
|---|---|---|
| 20° (normal) | ≥ 87 mm | 149 mm |
| 22° (hard stop) | ≥ 87 mm | 105 mm |
| 25° (no stop, for reference) | 41–45 mm | – |

The 22° stop is what keeps those margins. The 25° row shows why it's needed.

## 4. Controls and safety (sheet E9)

**One button (S3)** at the rear panel: hold DEPLOY or STOW. Movement only happens while it's held (dead-man).

| Step | Deploy | Stow |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check: ignition off, stowed (B1), flat (B3) | Allowed any time, ignition on too |
| 2 | Latches release | Tilt down to flat (B3) |
| 3 | Slide out until B2 | Latches release; slide back until B1 |
| 4 | Tilt to 20° (B4 set at 21°, stop at 22°) | Latches drop: springs lock the pins |
| 5 | Latches off; worm drives hold the panel | LED off = locked for travel |

- **Ignition interlock** (VIG, from the Orion's ignition sense): no deploy with the engine on. If the engine starts while the panel isn't stowed, buzzer BZ1 sounds and the LED flashes. Nothing moves on its own.
- **Slide only when flat:** M2 can run only with B3 closed.
- **Obstruction:** over-current on any channel stops the motion and backs off 2 mm.
- **Timeouts:** 25 s per step, then stop and flash a fault.
- **Wind:** stow when gusts exceed about 40 km/h. An anemometer could later go on a spare input for auto-alarm.

**Wiring:** FB1 way 10 (ATC 20 A) → VIS 6 mm² twin → controller U11 on the roof. VSW (3-core) runs from S3, VIG from the ignition sense. The panel feeds PVV (4 mm² high-flex in a cable chain) into the roof array's MC4 3-way branch.

## 5. Electrical changes

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **MPPT 100/30 → SmartSolar MPPT 100/50** | 570 W in parallel is ~42 A at 13.5 V, too much for a 30 A charger |
| F5 MIDI 40 A → **60 A** | Matches the 100/50 output |
| FB1 way 10 → visor drive, ATC 20 A | Gearmotor + two tilt drives + latches |
| New cables VIS, VSW, VIG, PVV | 50 cables, 125.3 m routed in total |
| New sheet **E9**; E3, E4, E6 and E1 updated | |

**Before buying the panel:** check its Voc is below ~25 V, so the three panels can run in parallel inside the 100 V / 50 A limits. Panels of similar Vmp also make the parallel array work best.

## 7. Measurements and decisions needed

1. **Roof crown:** height of the roof skin at the centre and at Y ±560, front and rear. The visor layer leaves only ~15 mm above the skin in the model.
2. **Platform:** height of the platform frame above the roof, and its front edge relative to the windscreen header.
3. **Windscreen:** header position and the glass angle (photos from the side help).
4. **Panel:** is ~1300 × 1000, ~250 W semi-flex on honeycomb right, or do you have a panel in mind?
5. **Roof load:** happy with ~15 kg left for the roof? If not, a lighter panel or rails would buy some back.
6. **Wind limit:** is "stow above ~40 km/h gusts" the rule you want?
7. **Buttons:** rear panel only, or also a dash switch for stowing from the cab?
