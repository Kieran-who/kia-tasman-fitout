## 2. Full wiring diagram

Sheets **E1–E8** are generated by `tools/wiring/diagram.py` from the same cable list that routes the 3D cables. Cable IDs and lengths always match `bom/cable-schedule-v0.8.csv`, and the generator fails if a cable isn't drawn.

| Sheet | Content |
|---|---|
| E1 | Locations, and which cables share each run (computed from the routed model) |
| E2 | Battery bank, MRBFs, busbars, ML-RBS isolation, SmartShunt, Class-T, MultiPlus DC, earthing |
| E3 | FB1 fuse block ways and every 12 V outlet |
| E4 | Alternator → Orion XS with ignition sense; roof and tent arrays → MPPTs |
| E5 | 230 V: shore inlet + RCBO → MultiPlus → RCBOs → outlets, earth path |
| E6 | Cerbo GX ports, GX Touch, Home Assistant, switch wiring, remote on/off |
| E7 | Tent harness, gap-junction connectors J1–J4 with pinouts, and the detach procedure |
| E8 | Cable routes (plan and side), bundles, cable to buy |

The schedules are on the same page: cables, protection, fuse-block ways and the component register.

**Design changes made while drawing it:**
- **Cerbo GX MK2:** it has only 3 VE.Direct ports. The Orion XS now connects through a **VE.Direct-to-USB** interface (D4).
- **ML-RBS:** its control circuit gets its own **2 A feed from the bank side** (F10/RBF), so the switch can close the RBS again. SW1 is now 4-core.
- **New circuits:**
  - IGN: ignition sense to the Orion's remote H, so smart-alternator voltage detection isn't relied on.
  - FAN: fused inverter fan.
  - CGX: Cerbo power.
  - PE1: inverter case to the system − bus (Victron: same size as the DC −).
  - CHB: one chassis bond.
- **Rear-panel DC outlets:** each now has its own fuse (DO1A–D) instead of sharing one cable.
- **Pod and cabin boxes:** each gets local 15 A and 10 A fuses for its 12 V and USB-C sockets.
- **Roof panels now in parallel.** The 190 W and 130 W panels have different currents, so in series the 190 W panel would be held back. Both arrays get 2-pole DC isolators.
- **MPPT 100/30 fuse:** reduced to 40 A.
- **Keyed tent connectors:**
  - The two 230 V connectors differ in rating and gender: J1 is a 10 A socket on the vehicle side, J2 is a 15 A inlet on the vehicle side. The tent's outlet can't be plugged into its own inlet.
  - The 12 V Anderson is red and the PV Anderson is grey; the two colours don't mate.
- **Shore power:** gets its own RCBO in the tent services box.

## 4. Layout issues the detail exposed (need decisions)

1. **MultiPlus cable entry.** Its cable-entry end is only **8 mm** from the distribution board, which was also true in v0.7. The 2 × 50 mm² DC cables and the AC glands need about 100 mm to bend.
   - Proposed fix: pass-through grommets in the board, in line with the inverter's entries, with the Class-T and busbars directly behind.
   - The alternative is re-shuffling the distribution column; the bay is about 90 mm short for that.
2. **DZ9308 front lock lever.** The real lever sticks out 27 mm, but there are only 2.6 mm to the rear plate. The model works the lever by Bowden cable from the rear pull. On the build: trim the lever or use a cable-release kit.
3. **Cable chain.** A real igus E2.26 with 38 mm inner width is 55 wide, and the lane is 50 mm. Use a chain no more than 40 mm wide outside (smaller series, ~25 mm inner width), or widen the lane, which costs deck width.
4. **Rear-panel GPO.** It needs a smaller twin, two single 56-series outlets, or a panel re-layout.
5. **Tent fold-out side.** The detail agent read `reference/vehicle-tent-open.jpg` as opening toward the passenger side. The model keeps the **driver side**, as you specified. Shout if the photo is right.
6. **Load-Lid gas struts.** They are modelled inside the top of the tub walls `[CONFIRM]`. If yours mount inside the tub, they would affect the passenger cable tray and the upper drawers.

**Also fixed:** the cable chain was 69 mm short at full extension. Its fixed end now sits at mid-travel, and its length is travel/2 + πR.
