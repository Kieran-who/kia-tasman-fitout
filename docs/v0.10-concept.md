# v0.10 concept: storage under the tent

Status: **tenth iteration, concept.** The interference check reports **0 clashes** (travel + camp). Bar positions, the tent bolt tails and the Destroyer rail height are still `[EST]`; the owner will send measurements.

Renders: [`renders/v0.10/`](../renders/v0.10)

## The space

- **Height:** 163 mm between the lid top and the tent's underside (owner measurement).
- **Under the Vortex bars:** about 133 mm.
- **Tent mounting bolts:** at the four spots where the tent's under-rails cross the bars (Y ±400), the bolt tails hang further down. The model assumes 60 mm `[EST]`; storage keeps 35 mm clear of those spots.
- **Destroyer rails** (~55 mm tall) along both lid edges, so nothing tall slides out sideways.
- **Access:** only the rear edge is fully open.

## Layout

| Item | Where | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| **Rivnut cross-bars** (5 × 40 × 5 mm flat bar, M8 rivnuts at 100 mm, same pitch as the slide decks) | Across the lid, clamped into the Destroyer rails' T-track | Bolt-on platform with no holes in the lid; costs 5 mm of height and ~4 kg |
| **30 L water** (OnTrack slimline, 340 × 860 × 150) | Front-passenger, lying across the vehicle; cap at the passenger end; two cam straps | It's 150 mm thick, so it can't pass under a bar or over a rail. It's **captive**: filled and drawn in place by hose. Check it's food-grade |
| **Rear-loading drawer** (cassette ~680 wide × 970 long × 117 high; drawer ~640 × 960 × 100) | Middle, between the tent-bolt lines, passing under the rear bar (16 mm clear) | Lockable; 36" full-extension locking runners; pulls out ~650+ mm behind the lid |
| **Wanderer folding table** (800 × 600 × 40 packed, 4.6 kg) | Flat on the drawer floor | Leaves ~50 mm above it for mats and other flat gear |
| **2 kg ABE fire extinguisher** | Rear-passenger corner, lying across the vehicle, quick-release strap bracket, head toward the passenger side | Reachable from behind or the passenger side in seconds, with nothing to unlock; also covers the tub (battery bay) below the tailgate |
| **RotopaX 1 gal (3.8 L) diesel** | Rear-driver corner on its pack mount | One heater refill; on the ladder/heater side, away from the water |
| Free bolt-on space | Both outer lanes between the bars (~440 × 460 mm each) | For later additions |
