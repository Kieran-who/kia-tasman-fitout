# v0.9: the v0.8 layout decisions, resolved for minimum space

Status: **ninth iteration.** The interference check reports **0 clashes** (travel + camp). The owner chose "whatever saves the most space" for each open v0.8 decision, and confirmed two facts:
- The tent folds open on the **driver side**.
- The Load-Lid comes off with a few bolts, so its gas struts aren't mounted inside the tub.

Tub photos and measurements will follow.

Renders: [`renders/v0.9/`](../renders/v0.9). Wiring: [`docs/wiring/`](wiring) (v0.9 HTML + PDF).

## Decisions

| v0.8 issue | v0.9 resolution (least space) | Space cost |
|---|---|---|
| MultiPlus cable entry only 8 mm from the distribution board | **Cable window in the lower distribution board** (240 × 100 mm, rubber edge grommet). The 2 × 50 mm² DC and the AC/comms cables pass straight through and bend in the ~115 mm gap before battery 1. The **system − busbar now lies flat on the base tray** just past the window, so the inverter's DC− lands on it in a straight line (`bay_02`, sheet E2) | none: bay size unchanged |
| Rear-panel GPO: no IP66 twin fits 120 × 86 | **Flush double GPO, Clipsal Iconic horizontal (120 × 78), the same part as the power pod, under a clear flip cover.** The panel sits 105 mm inside the tailgate and under the lid, so it is sheltered. Electrician to confirm the IP rating needed (`cabinets_01`) | none: fits the existing panel |
| igus E2.26 is 55 wide, the lane is 50 | **igus E2.15.30**: 30 mm inner × 14.4 high, 37.8 mm outer width. R75 for the chain-rated 2.5 mm² 3-core and 6 mm² twin; confirm that radius with igus (`slides_03`) | none: deck width stays 529 mm |
| DZ9308 front lock lever doesn't fit the 2.6 mm to the rear plate | **Cable release** from the rear pull (already modelled). Trim the lever on the build | none |
| Tent fold-out side | **Driver side** (owner confirmed) | – |
| Load-Lid gas struts | Outside the tub (lid removable with bolts). Modelled in the wall tops, clear of the fit-out | – |
