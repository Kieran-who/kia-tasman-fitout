## 1. Fold-out legs that live under the slides

**Yes.** The loose pin-in legs (previously kept in the utility drawer) are replaced by **two fold-out telescopic legs per slide**. They are permanently attached and stow under the deck.

**Where they fit**
- The only free space under each deck is between the last two carriage cross members: a bay 60–286 mm from the rear end, ~30 mm deep, under the cross members and above the cassette base.
- **Why they fold across, not lengthways:** on the power slide, the fridge well pan runs to within 3 mm of the side members for most of the length, so a full-length leg can't lie along the frame. Folding across the rear bay works on both slides, so **all four legs are the same part**.

**How they work**
1. **Pivot:** each leg hangs from a clevis (two 6 mm plates, M10 shoulder bolt) bolted through one RHS side member's inner face. One leg sits on each side member, offset in x (110 and 220 mm from the deck rear), so the two folded legs lie side by side.
2. **Stowed:** the leg folds inboard and lies across under the deck. A spring clip on a UHMW block under the deck holds it. Its underside sits 17 mm above the floor ribs, clear of the UHMW stops and base plates when stowed.
3. **Deploy:** pull the slide out, unclip the leg and swing it down 90°. A **ball-lock pin through the clevis** locks it vertical; the same 8 mm pins as the deck.
4. **Telescope:** three nested 6060 SHS sections, 32/25/19 mm, 380/370/360 long, with 8 mm holes at 25 mm pitch and two ball-lock pins. Fine adjustment is an M10 swivel levelling foot.
   - Closed length: 380 mm (+30 mm foot). That fits across the power deck's 429 mm inner width.
   - Reach: ~1000 mm below the pivot, with 70 mm overlap at each joint.

**Numbers**
- **Reach is the tight one.** The model assumes the tub floor is **920 mm above ground `[EST]`**, which leaves ~30 mm of extra reach plus the foot thread.
  - **Please measure ground → tub floor ribs at the tailgate.** If it's over ~940 mm, the fix is a 4th section or slightly longer sections on the driver slide, which is 50 mm wider.
- **Strength:** treating the whole leg as the thinnest (19 × 1.6) section over 1 m, Euler buckling is ~390 kg per leg. That's comfortably above the loads here. Joint slop matters more, so fit nylon or UHMW pads in each joint.
- **Mass:** ~0.7 kg per leg including clevis, foot and clip, about 3 kg for all four. That's about the same as the pin-in legs, and they free up space in the utility drawer.

**Tailgate catch:** the legs must land past the tip of the open tailgate. That needs **≥ ~70 % extension**.
- In the camp state the driver slide is out 65 %, so its legs stay folded. Partial extension relies on the runners' rating.
- The power slide is fully out, so its legs are down.

## 2. No bed hooks on the arches

- **What they were:** the v0.2–v0.6 model had four "factory bed hooks" as `[EST]` placeholders for the tub tie-down points: two on the arch inner faces ~720 mm from the front wall, two on the side walls near the tailgate. They weren't from a drawing or photo.
- **Now:** the owner's photo shows **no hooks on the arch faces, but a recessed well moulded into the arch**.
  - All four placeholders are removed.
  - Each arch now has a recess modelled as an `[EST]` 160 × 50 × 35 mm pocket at ~620–780 mm from the front wall, 195–245 mm above the ribs. **Measure it.**
  - A recess takes nothing from the tub, so nothing has to clear it.
- **Knock-on:** the 105 mm limit on edge fences at the driver arch is gone. The example fences are back to **150 mm**; anything up to ~180 mm clears the upper drawers when stowed.

The real tie-down points are still `[CONFIRM]`. Reviews mention adjustable tie-downs and anchor points on all four sides of the tub. If any sit low on the walls behind the arches, tell me where, because the control cabinet and utility drawer sit there.
