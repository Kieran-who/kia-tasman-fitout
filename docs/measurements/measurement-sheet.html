Vehicle measurement sheet

Every dimension the 3D model still guesses: 94 items in nine areas. Fill these in, send back a photo of the pages (or the CSV), and the model gets updated. Then the cutting lists can be trusted. Each item shows what the model assumes now. A big difference from that is exactly what we need to catch. The Stage column shows the first build stage that depends on it (see docs/build-stages.md), and the 📷 box is for ticking off a photo.

Before you start

  • Park on level ground, normal tyre pressures, unladen, and note the fuel level.
  • Everything is in mm, measured to the liner surface (what the furniture will touch) unless it says otherwise.
  • Driver side = right (D), passenger side = left (P).
  • Run masking tape along the tub centreline (half the wall-to-wall width, marked at the front and at the tailgate). That's Y = 0.
  • "Front / mid / rear" in the tub means X ≈ 100, 750 and 1400 unless an item says otherwise.
  • Photograph each item with the tape in shot and its ID written on masking tape.
  • Measure twice. If two readings differ by more than 2 mm, write both.

Tools

  • 5 m tape and a 1 m steel rule
  • 300 mm steel rule and digital calipers
  • Straight edge ≥ 1.8 m (an aluminium level is ideal) and a spirit level
  • Digital angle finder (a phone app works)
  • Plumb bob or a laser line, string line
  • Masking tape, marker, a radius gauge or a few coins

Priority

  • Stages 1 and 3 (tub, arches, fittings, chassis): needed before cutting anything for the battery bay, cabinets and slides.
  • Stage 6 (lid, bars, tent): under-tent storage.
  • Stages 2 and 7 (roof, windscreen): platform and visor.

Photo checklist

  • Whole tub from the tailgate, from each rear corner and from above (lid open)
  • Each arch: side on, with a tape along it; close-up of each arch well with a rule in it
  • Front wall and both front corners; the floor ribs with a rule across them
  • Factory outlet: on, and removed showing the hole and what's behind
  • Every tie-down and protrusion with a rule beside it
  • Under the lid: hinges, lock, struts, clamps, any frame members
  • Destroyer rail end-on (T-slot profile) with calipers
  • Each of the 4 tent-bolt crossings from the side, with a rule from the bar to the lowest point
  • Cab–tub gap from above and below; chassis rail beside the gap
  • Roof: straight edge across with the gaps visible; the roof mounting points uncovered
  • Side profile of the windscreen and bonnet with a level for reference
  • Engine bay: start battery, fuse box, route to the chassis; under the rear seat
