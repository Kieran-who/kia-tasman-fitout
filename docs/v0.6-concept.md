## 1. Receivers closer to the edges

**The grid now runs to the edges of each deck.**

| | Power slide (529 wide) | Driver slide (579 wide) |
|---|---|---|
| Edge rows (long sides) | **37.6 mm** in from each edge | **37.6 mm** in from each edge |
| Interior rows | ±50 and ±150 mm from the L-track | ±50 and ±150 mm from the L-track |
| Front row | 135 mm from the deck front (behind the power pod) | **35 mm** from the deck front |
| Rear row | **35 mm** from the deck rear | **35 mm** from the deck rear |
| Long pitch | 100 mm (last bay 104 mm) | 100 mm (last bay 104 mm) |
| Receivers | 34 on the deck + 32 in the well filler | 72 |

**Why the edge rows sit at 37.6 mm.** That point is the centre of the RHS side member under the deck:
- The pin passes through the 15 mm ply and the L-angle's top leg, then through the RHS top wall. Its balls lock **underneath the RHS top wall**, which grips far better than ply alone.
- Any closer and the pin would land over the runner, which sits only ~2 mm under the deck lip. There's no room there for a pin tip.

**Swapping modules between slides.** The interior rows are the same on both slides, so any module pinned only to interior rows fits either slide. The edge rows differ (529 vs 579 wide), so full-width edge modules are made per slide.

**The front and rear rows sit over the end cross members**, so modules can run to the ends of the deck too.

**Pins:** use 8 mm ball-lock pins with **≥ 32 mm grip** (15 ply + 3 angle + RHS wall, plus the ball reach). The interior pins hang into free space under the deck.

## 2. Flush dividers / edge fences

**A divider can't have a pin in its own wall line at the very edge.** So the wall sits on a foot that runs inboard over the edge row:

```
        12 mm ply wall, flush with the deck edge
        ┃
        ┃
  ━━━━━━┫  60 x 6 alu flat foot (pins at 37.6 mm from the edge)
  ══════╧═══ deck edge
     ↓ pin into the RHS side member
```

- **Modelled examples:** a full-length fence along both long edges of each deck, plus one cross divider per deck. The cross divider's foot sits between the two fence feet and is pinned to the interior rows.
- **Height:** the examples are **95 mm**. When stowed, the front factory bed hooks on the driver arch face stick ~26 mm into the deck-edge zone from ~108 mm above the deck, at x ≈ 720.
  - Anything flush to the **driver outboard edge** at that point must stay under ~105 mm when stowed, **or unbolt those two hooks**. They're unusable with the lid shut anyway.
  - Taller fences are fine everywhere else.
- **Mass:** about 10 kg for the six example pieces. They're optional and aren't counted in the fit-out total.

## 3. Fridge-well filler (no fridge fitted)

**Yes.** A removable filler turns the well into flush deck:

- **15 mm ply panel, 758 × 415**, with 4 mm clearance all round. It carries **its own L-track section and the same receiver grid** (32 receivers), so the whole power slide reads as one continuous surface.
- **It sits on two 18 mm ply risers (706 × 65)** that stand on the well's L-track strips and stud into them with L-track studs. That locates the filler and stops it lifting. Lift it out to fit a fridge.
- **The filler top is exactly flush with the deck top.**
- **Mass:** 5.0 kg. It's an option, not counted in the fit-out total. The cut list has it under `well_filler`.

Fridge limits are unchanged: **≤ ~756 × 420 mm footprint, ≤ ~526 mm tall** standing on the well tracks.
