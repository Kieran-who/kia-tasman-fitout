# v0.5 concept: side-mount runners with a covered, continuous deck

Status: **fifth iteration.** The interference check reports **0 clashes** (travel + camp). The Rollon under-mount design (v0.4) was dropped on cost, and the slides are back on Accuride DZ9308 side-mount runners, now **covered**.

Renders: [`renders/v0.5/`](../renders/v0.5). See `06_section` for the build-up and `02` / `04` / `10` for the empty decks.

## How the covered deck works

```
   15 mm ply deck (continuous over both slides, 6 mm gap in the middle)
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐   top  ~102 mm
 │══════════════════════════════════════════════│   ply   87–102
 └─┬───────────┬──────────────────┬───────────┬─┘
   │◄ L-angle  │                  │  L-angle ►│     60x40x3 top leg reaches over the runner, 84–87
   │  over the │ RHS 75x25 side   │           │
 ▐▌│  runner   │ member (moving)  │           │▐▌   DZ9308 runner (19 mm), lock in + lock out
 ▐▌│           │                  │           │▐▌   fixed runner plate (to 82 mm, 2 mm under the angle)
═══════════════ base plates on the floor ribs ═══════════════
```

- **Each moving frame** is two RHS 75×25 side members on the runners plus RHS cross members, the same as v0.3.
- **The deck carrier** is an aluminium **L-angle (60 × 40 × 3)** along each side member. Its vertical leg bolts to the side member's inner face, and its top leg reaches out **over the runner to the deck edge**. The ply screws to the angles and cross members.
- **Coverage:** each deck covers its whole slide, runners included. **The two decks meet in the middle with a 6 mm gap**, giving one flat surface across the tub. Modules on the two slides can butt together, and the receiver grid runs edge to edge.
- **The only opening is the fridge well** at the front of the power slide. The fridge drops to 22 mm (on two L-track strips in a 3 mm pan), leaving **~526 mm clear** above them for a ~476 mm fridge.
  - The well is 766 mm long and ~425 mm wide, so **the fridge must be ≤ ~756 × 420 mm** (Dometic CFX3 45 at ~720 × 398 fits).
- **Height:** deck top **~102 mm** (v0.3 was 84). The ply has to clear the fixed runner tops, so the covered surface costs ~18 mm of headroom, leaving **446 mm** clear under the lid on the deck.
- **Cable chain:** with no centre gap, the upright igus chain moves to a **50 mm lane on the power slide's outboard side**, against the passenger arch / control cabinet. Its bend radius is now 63 mm to suit chain-rated 2.5 mm² and 6 mm² cable. Its upper run is bracketed to the deck edge.
- **Unchanged:** DZ9308 lock in and lock out, UHMW stops when stowed (road shock bypasses the runners), pin-in legs at full extension, and the power pod with up-facing sockets.
