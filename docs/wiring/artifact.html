E1

System overview: locations and cable bundles

Where each part lives and which cables share each run. Bundles are worked out from the routed 3D model. Circuits on sheets E2–E7 and E9.

E2

DC power: battery bank and main distribution

Front bay, between the wheel arches. 12.8 V LiFePO₄ bank → isolation → protection → system busbars.

E3

DC distribution: fuse block and 12 V outlets

FB1 in the rear control cabinet feeds every 12 V outlet. Twin cables: + from the numbered way, − to the FB1 negative bus.

E4

Charging: alternator (DC-DC) and solar

Three independent chargers on the front-wall board, each with its own MIDI fuse to the system busbars (sheet E2).

E5

230 V AC: shore inlet, inverter output, RCBOs and outlets

Licensed electrician to install and certify (AS/NZS 3000, AS/NZS 3001.2). Active brown · neutral blue · earth green/yellow.

E6

Control and communications

Cerbo GX gathers every Victron device for the GX Touch, Home Assistant and VRM. Hard-wired switches at the rear panel.

E7

Detachable tent harness and gap junction

Four keyed connectors at the cab–tub gap. Unplug J1–J4, cap them, and the tent lifts off with its harness and services box.

E8

Cable routes: plan and side view

Generated from the routed 3D model (travel state). Every cable leaves the tub through the factory-outlet gland.

E9

Visor drive: slide, tilt and locking

The windscreen visor panel (PV5) slides out on two rails and tilts to 20°. One button at the rear panel; every motion is interlocked.

Before power-up

  • Measure the real cable runs: these lengths come from [EST] positions in the model.
  • Confirm the ML-RBS control-wire colours and switch wiring against the Blue Sea 7713 instructions.
  • Set every charger to iTechworld's LiFePO₄ voltages; set the Orion XS input limit to 25–30 A.
  • Torque every stud, then re-check after the first corrugated road.
  • Electrician: earth continuity, polarity, RCD trip tests on shore power and in inverter mode.
