Fit-out wiring diagram

Sixteen drawing sheets and the full schedules for the 12 V and 230 V systems. E1–E7, E9 and E16 are the circuits: battery bank, isolation and protection, DC distribution, charging, the inverter's AC side, control, the detachable tent harnesses, the visor drive and the cold-weather heating (battery and water heaters, charge cut-off below +3 °C). E8 and E10–E15 are the cable routes, drawn to scale from the routed 3D model: every run (R-code) with its containment and fixing, every cable labelled in every zone, and every bundle's cross-section and conduit fill. Cable IDs match bom/cable-schedule-v0.14.csv; runs match bom/route-schedule-v0.14.csv.

E1

System overview: locations and cable bundles

Where each part lives and which cables share each run (from the model's runs, E8). Circuits on E2–E7, E9 and E16; routes to scale on E8, E10–E15.

E2

DC power: battery bank and main distribution

Front bay, between the wheel arches. 12.8 V LiFePO₄ bank → isolation → protection → system busbars.

E3

DC distribution: fuse block and 12 V outlets

FB1 in the rear control cabinet feeds every 12 V outlet. Twin cables: + from the numbered way, − to the FB1 negative bus.

E4

Charging: alternator (DC-DC) and solar

Four independent chargers in the front bay, each output fused at the system busbars (sheet E2).

E5

230 V AC: shore inlet, inverter output, RCBOs and outlets

Licensed electrician to install and certify (AS/NZS 3000, AS/NZS 3001.2). Active brown · neutral blue · earth green/yellow.

E6

Control and communications

Cerbo GX gathers every Victron device for the GX Touch, Home Assistant and VRM. Hard-wired switches at the rear panel.

E7

Detachable tent harness and gap junction

Four keyed connectors at the cab–tub gap. Unplug J1–J4, cap them, and the tent lifts off with its harness and services box.

E8

Cable routes: route map and run schedule

Every physical run (R-code) from the routed 3D model. Detail by zone on E10–E14; bundle sections and fill on E15.

E9

Visor drive: slide, tilt and locking

The windscreen visor panel (PV5) slides out on two rails and tilts to 20°. One button at the rear panel; every motion is interlocked.

E10

Cable routes: front bay

Busbar side, rear channel (behind the board edge and the inverter), front channel, charging-board loom and the exits to the tray and the slide.

E11

Cable routes: tray, wall channel, gland plate and rear cabinet

Passenger side of the tub. 230 V (orange) on the wall side of the tray divider, its own drop, gland row and cabinet gland.

E12

Cable routes: wall cavity, chassis rail, cabin and engine bay

Outside the tub: 12 V in black split conduit, 230 V in orange conduit below it on the rail web; P-clips every 300 mm.

E13

Cable routes: cab–tub gap, junction box, roof and tent harnesses

Risers up the gap to the junction box (230 V and 12 V sides), on up the cab back to the platform's hollow rear end member; tent harnesses across the lid front.

E14

Cable routes: power slide (trunk and chain)

AC3 (230 V) and DO2 (12 V) leave the bay under the guard, run on the glide tray, then through the chain (separator between them) to the pod.

E15

Bundle cross-sections and fill

Every run's cross-section as packed in the model (biggest cables first; twins as two cores). Dashed circle = conduit inside diameter.

E16

Cold-weather heating: battery bank and water can

Snow camping (−5 to −15 °C nights): keep the LiFePO₄ bank above 0 °C so it may charge, stop every charger below +3 °C, and keep the 30 L can from freezing.

Before power-up

  • Measure the real cable runs: these lengths come from [EST] positions in the model.
  • Confirm the ML-RBS control-wire colours and switch wiring against the Blue Sea 7713 instructions.
  • Set every charger to iTechworld's LiFePO₄ voltages; set the Orion XS input limit to 25–30 A.
  • Torque every stud, then re-check after the first corrugated road.
  • Electrician: earth continuity, polarity, RCD trip tests on shore power and in inverter mode.
